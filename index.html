Using the timer

A phone may overheat in a sauna. You can leave your phone outside, use the timer when convenient, and adjust any recorded durations afterward.

Your timer uses timestamps and saves its draft locally, so locking your phone or switching apps won't lose your elapsed time. Pause it during breaks you don't want counted.
